## Steps

1. Entry: Sales Management (销售管理) → Sales Center (销售中心) → **Call Records (通话记录)** tab.
2. Search by user ID / name / login account, or filter by country, **call outcome**, outbound call agent email, and time range.
3. Verify: call start time, user ID, name, phone number, call outcome, duration, Recording (录音), Transcript (文字稿), and follow-up record for this call.
4. For calls marked Connected (已接通), click **Play recording (播放录音)**; where a transcript exists, click **Download transcript (下载文字稿)**.

## Field notes

- **Call outcome**: Connected (已接通) / No answer (无人接听), etc.
- **Recording**: shows Play recording or Syncing (recordings of recently ended calls need time to sync).
- **Transcript**: shows Download transcript or Recording not yet available.

## Confirm completion

You can locate the target call and decide the next step.

## Notes

- Recordings of calls that just ended may show Syncing; refresh later.
- Regular sales reps can usually view only their own call records.
