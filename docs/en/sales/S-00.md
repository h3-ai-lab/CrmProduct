## Page structure

Entry: Sales Management (销售管理) → Sales Center (销售中心). A banner at the top shows the full lead flow:

> Registered (with phone number) → Auto assignment / Pending claim → Follow-up in progress → On payment, returns to User Center

![My Follow-ups in the Sales Center](images/S-00-myfollow.png)

## Lead flow rules (click View Details for the original text)

- Registered users who **have a phone number and have not paid** automatically enter the Sales Center.
- If the user matches a business line assignment weight, they go directly to Follow-up in progress; otherwise they go to Pending claim (Lead Pool (公海)).
- Users **unpaid after 30 days** or manually returned to the Lead Pool must be claimed manually again.
- Leads leave the Sales Center automatically once the user pays.
- The system does not support manual phone number entry.

![Flow rules detail](images/S-00-flow.png)

## Four tabs

| Tab | Content | Related manual |
|---|---|---|
| Lead Pool | Leads pending claim; row action is Claim & Follow up (领取跟进) | S-01 |
| My Follow-ups (我的跟进) | Leads under your name; row actions are Dial (拨号) + More (更多) (Trial Lesson Report / Update follow-up / Reassign lead / Add membership duration) | S-02 / S-03 / S-04 |
| Paid (已付费) | Users who have paid (user status = Paid); dialing and follow-up also available | S-02 |
| Call Records (通话记录) | All outbound call records and recordings | S-05 |

## Common filters and actions

- Filters (筛选器): search by phone number / user ID / name, country, purchase intent, CC, current follow-up stage, age group, course level, channel source - Landing Page, channel source - App, user status, sales consultation booking, booking outbound call, registration date range.
- Toolbar buttons: **Reset filters**, **Import leads**, **Download data**.

## Team lead capabilities

After logging in, a Sales Lead (销售组长) sees a notice: "You can view and reassign follow-up leads of all sales reps in your team (within your business line scope)", and can use **Assignment & auto-release settings** in the top-right corner (see A-02).
