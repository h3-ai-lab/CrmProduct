## Page structure

Entry: **Sales Management (销售管理) → Sales Center (销售中心)**.

The top flow banner shows:

> Registered (with phone number) → Auto assignment / pending claim → Follow-up → Paid users stay available in CRM

The page has four tabs:

| Tab | What it shows | Common actions |
|---|---|---|
| Lead Pool | Leads waiting to be claimed | Claim & Follow up |
| My Follow-ups | Your current leads | Dial, update follow-up, reassign, return to pool |
| Paid | Paid users | Dial and continue renewal follow-up |
| Call Records | Call result, recording and transcript | Play recording, download transcript |

## Lead flow

1. Registered users with a phone number and no payment enter Sales Center.
2. Users matching business-line weights go directly to a rep's My Follow-ups.
3. Users without a match enter the Lead Pool for manual claiming.
4. If no follow-up is recorded within the configured time, a lead may be auto-released and reassigned.
5. Leads unpaid for 30 days or manually returned go back to the pool.
6. After payment, users appear in the Paid tab and currently remain with the rep for renewal follow-up.
7. Manual phone-number entry is not supported; new lead sources follow the existing ingestion process.

## Common filters

- Phone number / user ID / name.
- Country, purchase intent, CC and current follow-up stage.
- Age range and course level.
- Landing-page source, App source and channel code.
- User status, consultation booking, outbound-call booking and registration date.

## Toolbar

- **Reset filters**: clear current conditions.
- **Import leads**: bulk lead import; permission required.
- **Download data**: download the filtered set; permission and authorised use required.
- **Assignment & auto-release settings**: normally hidden from Sales Reps; available to Sales Leads and admins.

## Sales Lead view

Sales Leads can view and reassign leads within their team and business-line scope, and configure assignment and auto-release rules in the top-right corner.

## Notes

- Purchase intent and follow-up stage support prioritisation; they are not proof of conversion.
- Paid users currently remain in Sales Center for renewal follow-up.
- Phone numbers, recordings, imports and exports are sensitive.
