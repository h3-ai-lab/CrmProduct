## Steps

1. Entry: System Management (系统管理) → System Configuration (系统配置) → **Member Accounts (成员账号)** tab.
2. Click **+ Add Member (+ 新增成员)**, fill in the email and nickname, and assign a role and business line (data scope (数据权限) follows the role).
3. For existing members: search by email/nickname, or filter by role and status, then click **Edit Member (编辑成员)** to modify information.
4. For call center agents, click **Bind Outbound Calling (绑定外呼)** (already-bound rows show **Manage Outbound Calling (管理外呼)**) to complete the phone bar account association (for usage on the sales side see S-02).
5. When a member leaves or is inactive for a long time, switch their status to **Disabled (停用)**.

## Completion check

The new member is visible in the member list with correct status and business line; agent rows show the bound outbound calling badge (CC).

## Notes

- Roles determine functional scope and business lines determine data scope; both must be assigned correctly.
- Disabling does not delete historical follow-ups and Call Records; data becomes visible again automatically after re-enabling.
- Every add/edit/enable-disable of a member enters the Audit Log (操作日志) (see A-05).
