## Page structure

Entry: left menu → **Dashboard (管理看板)**.

The page has three tabs:

| Tab | What it answers |
|---|---|
| Conversion overview | Six-stage conversion and lead-to-paid rate for a registration cohort |
| Sales follow-up | Current leads, calls, bookings, follow-ups and rejection/closure reasons |
| Payment results | Paid users, orders, paid amount, AOV and currency |

## Review conversion

1. Select the business line, CC and registration date.
2. Review the six stages: leads (including paid) → called users → connected users → booked users → trial completions → paid users.
3. Review **lead-to-paid rate (L2S)** and its numerator/denominator.
4. Open **Metric definitions** in the top-right corner for the current definitions.

## Read the chart

- The registration date selects one cohort; later events are counted up to the present.
- Each stage is deduplicated by user ID.
- A cohort may skip stages, so counts do not have to decrease at every step.
- Trial completions come from course records, not sales attendance.
- Payments use valid paid orders; currencies are not added across currencies.

## Use Sales Follow-up

1. Start with the current lead overview.
2. Review pending calls, connected follow-ups and pending bookings.
3. Compare called and connected users to identify route, time-window or script issues.
4. Review rejection and closure reasons to identify recurring objections.

## Notes

- Displayed dates and update time use the current browser time zone.
- Keep business line, CC and date scope consistent when comparing trends.
- The dashboard is for analysis and verification; it does not edit leads or orders.
