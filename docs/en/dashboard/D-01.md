## Steps

1. Entry: left menu → **Dashboard (管理看板)**.
2. Select a business line (业务线) (e.g. Malaysia), CC, and registration date range; the three cards at the top are: **Conversion overview** (six-stage conversion for the same registration cohort), **Sales follow-up** (outbound calls and follow-up status), and **Payment results** (paid users, revenue, and AOV).
3. Review the Registration cohort conversion funnel below: 01 lead count (incl. Paid) → 02 users called → 03 users connected → 04 users booked → 05 trial completed → 06 users paid, with the lead-to-paid conversion rate summarized on the right.

## Definitions (original page text)

- The registration date selects one cohort of leads; subsequent results are tracked up to the present.
- Each stage is **deduplicated by user**; users from the same registration cohort may skip some stages, so counts do not necessarily decrease stage by stage (for example, trial completions come from course records and are counted separately from sales booking attendance).

## Confirm completion

The funnel and conversion rates for the selected business line display correctly and match recent outbound call cadence.

## Notes

- The funnel is the first entry point for admins to spot bottlenecks: when bookings are low relative to connected calls, check outbound call time slots and scripts first (see S-02 / A-04).
- Dashboard data updates with the statistical definitions; keep filters consistent when comparing multi-day trends.
